A CLOSER LOOK

What happens after launch?

Launching an ad is only the beginning. We check whether people notice the message, whether they take the intended action and whether the cost fits your business goal. Changes are made from evidence, not one-day swings.

01

Read the signals

Reach and click-through rate help assess the creative; landing-page visits and conversion events help show what happens after the click.

02

Check tracking

We verify that key events such as leads, purchases or form submissions are recorded as accurately as the setup allows.

03

Improve one variable at a time

We compare creatives, audiences and landing-page paths so the team can understand which change may have helped.

04

Report the next action

Reports summarize spend, outcomes, learning and the next test. Results vary by offer, market, budget and website experience.
